Surabaya

The City of Heroes

Surabaya has a long history dating back to the 13th century, when it developed as an important settlement and trading port in East Java. The city became increasingly influential under the Majapahit Kingdom and later grew as a major port during the colonial period. Surabaya is also famous for the Battle of Surabaya in November 1945, when Indonesian fighters fought British and Allied forces after Indonesia declared independence. This event became an important symbol of Indonesian independence and is commemorated every year on Heroes' Day (Hari Pahlawan).


Interesting Facts

- Mall culture is popular — Because of Surabaya's hot weather, many people enjoy spending their free time at shopping malls, which offer shopping, restaurants, entertainment, and air conditioning all in one place.
- It can get really hot — Surabaya has a tropical climate, and daytime temperatures can regularly reach around 30–34°C, especially during the dry season.
- One of Indonesia's hotter major cities — Surabaya is known for its warm and humid weather compared with many other Indonesian cities.
- The city has a unique symbol — Surabaya's name is traditionally associated with a shark (sura) and a crocodile (baya), which appear together in the city's famous emblem.
- Food is a big part of the culture — Surabaya is famous for local dishes such as rawon, rujak cingur, lontong balap, and tahu tek.
- A major port city — Surabaya has long been an important center for trade because of its location on the northeastern coast of Java.

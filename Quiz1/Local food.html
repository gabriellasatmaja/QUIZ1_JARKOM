Surabaya Cuisine

Discover the flavors of Surabaya

Most popular

Rujak Cingur

Rujak cingur is a traditional Surabaya dish made with slices of beef snout (cingur), vegetables, tofu, tempeh, and fruit such as cucumber and pineapple. It is covered in a thick blackish-brown sauce made from peanuts, chili, and petis (a fermented shrimp paste). It has a savory, sweet, spicy, and slightly salty flavor with a mix of crunchy, fresh, and chewy textures.

Lontong Balap

Lontong balap is made with sliced rice cakes (lontong), bean sprouts, fried tofu, and lentho, served with a savory and slightly sweet sauce. It has a fresh, crunchy texture from the bean sprouts.

Sate Klopo

Sate klopo is grilled meat skewers coated with grated coconut before being grilled. The coconut gives the meat a fragrant, slightly sweet, and savory flavor, with a lightly charred outside.

Tahu Campur

Tahu campur is a mixed dish containing tofu, beef, noodles, vegetables, and crispy crackers in a savory broth. It usually has a rich, slightly sweet and savory flavor.

Rawon

Rawon is a traditional East Javanese beef soup with a distinctive dark black broth made from keluak. It has a rich, savory, slightly earthy flavor and is usually served with rice, bean sprouts, and salted egg.

Watch Video

Tahu Tek

Tahu tek consists of fried tofu, potatoes, bean sprouts, and sometimes cucumber, topped with a thick peanut sauce. The sauce gives it a sweet, savory, and slightly spicy taste.
